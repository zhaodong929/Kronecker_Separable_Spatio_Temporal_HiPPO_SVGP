## Verdict

The comparison is repairable without new baselines, an ablation suite, or blindly discarding every completed result. The concrete implementation blockers are adaptive OSGPR amplitude continuation and long-domain MGPVAE prediction/selection reproducibility. PEMS additionally needs one shared physical time convention. The major manuscript blocker is interpreting delayed COVID/PEMS multi-geometry inference as empirical execution of the single-Sylvester cubic theorem. These are separate problems with different remedies.

The best minimal contract preserves the already authorized three problem families, describes them as contemporaneous partial-observation spatial prediction, and narrows solver claims to their actual assumptions. Changing COVID/PEMS release rules merely to obtain a single spatial Gram matrix would be a scientifically different task requiring all affected methods to rerun. The current singleton PEMS cadence is valid; renaming groups of updates as Tasks would neither repair geometry nor save those updates.

## Horizontal contract: what is common and what need not be

| Axis | Must agree among five methods within a dataset | Can differ if explicitly disclosed and independently validated |
|---|---|---|
| Information | Initial legal target set, current visible observations, delayed-release event/order, covariate availability, query targets | How legally available information is compressed or optimized |
| Time | Physical model-time coordinate and query/release timestamps | Temporal kernel, normalized units with corresponding parameter interpretation, separate calendar features |
| Initialization/selection | Fit/validation boundary, legal historical tuning, no final stream tuning, normalization source | Optimizers, capacity candidates, stopping rules, use of analytic conjugacy |
| Mean and model | Common availability of mean inputs and disclosure of fitted data | Joint Bayesian online beta versus fixed ridge offset; nonlinear residual decoder; uncertainty representation |
| Evaluation | Same queried points, observation-space proper scores, target units, pooled calibration definition | Gaussian analytic scores versus correctly approximated nonlinear mixture scores |
| Resource claims | Decision-to-ready-prediction boundary, comparable hardware/competition and memory definitions | Number of internal optimizer operations and legitimate method-specific state dimension |

“Same information” is necessary but not sufficient for a strong benchmark: implausibly restrictive capacity/tuning choices could still weaken baselines. Conversely, different update counts, dimensions, kernels, or mean models are not by themselves proof of unfairness. Report the chosen operating points and validation evidence; avoid claims of globally optimized superiority.

Across datasets the contracts intentionally differ: ERA5 never releases held-out targets; COVID initially sees every jurisdiction and subsequently releases hidden labels after one week; PEMS initially excludes hidden sensors and subsequently releases them after one sample. This gives useful coverage of three inference situations, not three IID replicates of a single unseen-location forecasting task. Scores have different units and must not be numerically averaged across domains. Millions of dependent site-time observations are not millions of scientific replicates.

## Adversarial corrections to possible overstatements

1. **OSGPR defect is real, but its meaning is precise.** Independently read `scripts/run_official_bui_osgpr_era5.py:64`–82 and `:140`–147: all product amplitudes are trainable but only temporal amplitude is restored. The official streaming objective mathematically permits new kernels; the problem is an undocumented involuntary reset, which invalidates the intended learned-parameter continuation and selection comparison. It is not proof that Bui's method or every old floating-point prediction is undefined.
2. **Selection interior is not global convergence.** Proposal's observed COVID/PEMS patience stops make nominal budget truncation an unsupported criticism of those runs. MGPVAE's iteration-1 choice and OHSVGP's iteration-5 choice are legitimate early selections under their rules. Do not upgrade any interior checkpoint into proof of optimizer convergence. Conversely, do not reject an early checkpoint after seeing final performance.
3. **Capacity boundary is a limitation, not an automatic failure certificate.** A declared finite grid can legitimately choose its largest candidate. It cannot establish that larger capacity would not help. Equal raw M across OHSVGP, product-inducing proposal, and latent MGPVAE would not resolve this. Predeclare/report capacity and resource budgets; require additional within-method tuning only when necessary to substantiate the intended adequacy claim, not an unbounded search.
4. **Fixed versus adaptive is not an information-boundary violation.** Proposal's learned beta and baseline fixed means prevent solver-only attribution; they do not automatically force all methods to freeze beta or optimize identical parameters. MGPVAE's shared offset means its inclusion alone does not prove the necessity of a mean-plus-GP decomposition. Limit the claim to the compared systems and cite the model rationale separately.
5. **Bounded state is not bounded executable memory.** Archived predictions and growing checkpoints need O(T) storage without disproving constant-dimensional sufficient statistics. Conversely, state-byte counters do not establish constant process memory or full-update cost. Proposal temporal-factor generation also has work outside the core solver; report complete observed costs.
6. **Timing corrections are generally reaggregation, not accuracy reruns.** Proposal PEMS factor/update/prediction sum is approximately 4351 s, not 1683 s. ST PEMS 1327 s is final refit, not selection exploration; its 110 s residual includes logging/checkpoints, not pure initial filtering. These corrected scalars still do not establish a publication-quality cross-method speed ratio without hardware and scope controls.
7. **Source-aligned completed is not admitted.** Shared archived timestamp arrays can conceal different actual model clocks. Literal `current_hidden_labels_read=0` metadata is not an instrumented leakage test. File residency of hidden targets likewise is not leakage unless predictions depend on them before release.
8. **MGPVAE root cause is unresolved.** Eager GPU/CPU agreement and compiled failure motivate the no-outer-JIT repair, but are not a demonstrated compiler-bug diagnosis. Long-domain finite qualification must pass before restarting that route; demanding every possible trained state be certified is unnecessary.

## OHSVGP interpolation and final refit: minimum defensible qualification

Independent source inspection: `basis_grid` (`scripts/run_covid_ohsvgp_own_theta.py:249`) selects up to 1024 input rows without inspecting labels. The official `HIPPOOSVGP` accepts `Z_interpolate` as its input representation and `get_Z` integrates features of those supplied rows (`baselines/external/harrisonzhu508_HIPPOSVGP/hipposvgp/multidim.py:94`). The adapter refits the variational posterior from minibatches of the entire legally initial population, then records `previous_steps=len(basis_grid(...))` (`run_covid_ohsvgp_own_theta.py:457`–504). Subsequent features extend that compressed row stream with actual observation rows.

This defines a valid discrete inducing-functional representation using an initialization grid. It is not automatically an implementation bug, and it is not necessary for those inducing functionals to equal integration over every training label to define a sparse GP. It is also not equivalent to uniform full-prefix row integration: the initialization measure and later row measure differ. “Official core with a 1024-row initialization interpolation grid, followed by arrival-row integration” is accurate; “unchanged temporal HiPPO integration of the entire history” is not.

Minimum finite evidence before strong fidelity claims: at small dimensions and fixed frequencies, compare the adapter's lazy continuation on the exact selected initialization grid plus subsequent rows against direct pinned-official recurrence on that same concatenated grid/row sequence. Compare the resulting feature state, Kuu/Kuf, and prediction/transport with regularization held fixed. Existing lazy-transition and cache tests are useful but do not by themselves demonstrate this complete compressed-prefix handoff. This is a bounded numerical qualification, not a new scientific ablation or a requirement to sweep grid sizes on final tests. If the intended baseline is instead integration over the entire physical observation history, replace the approximation and rerun affected selection/finals.

The final posterior is freshly trained for the selected validation iteration count with kernel/noise fixed. Five minibatches cannot literally assimilate every one of COVID's 2704 initial rows, but stochastic initialization does not require every datum to be visited for its declared finite-training predictor to be defined. Reusing the selected training duration is common; changing from jointly optimized calibration to frozen-kernel fresh-state refit does make an explicit qualification useful. Inspect legal Task-1 objective/finite diagnostics and the declared initialization policy; do not call it fully converged or exact all-row assimilation. If the policy is changed to a separately converged refit, regenerate finals (and any online-budget selection using that altered initialization). Do not silently change only the weakest observed split.
